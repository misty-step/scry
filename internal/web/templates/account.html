Private access

Every application request requires the approved private identity. Scry does not store browser history snapshots or an offline answer queue.

To sign out or switch accounts, use exe's account controls for this application address. Scry cannot sign out your upstream exe account from here.

Archiving is not erasure. It stops future selection while preserving historical evidence and backups. Full erasure and backup retention are separate operator decisions.
