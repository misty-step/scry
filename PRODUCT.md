# Scry

Scry gets what's worth knowing into your head and keeps it there, for the fewest minutes a day. It is built for Phaedrus and usable by anyone.

## User stories

- As a newcomer, I can understand at a glance that Scry is an AI-judged active-recall instrument, see one real example of a card being judged on the landing page, sign up and sign in through Clerk with email and password, and be studying within a minute: the 7-day trial starts at once, with no card up front. I can also add Scry to my phone's home screen to launch directly into daily recall.
- As a learner on the trial, I always know how many days are left, and I can add a card any time in Billing or on the card step to keep Scry after it; adding one during the trial charges nothing until the trial ends. When the trial ends or its allowance runs out, Scry says so calmly and shows me where to add a card.
- As a newcomer, I must verify my email before I can study or use billing.
- As a returning learner who forgot my password, I enter my email on Sign in, choose "Forgot password" on the password step to reset it by email, and sign in with my new password.
- As a new user with an empty library, I can pick from about 30 excellent starter sets and start right away. The shelf leads with sets anyone can enjoy at once (the NATO phonetic alphabet, mental models, kitchen numbers, world geography), then core ideas in every major subject and vocabulary; the Creeds and the other word-for-word texts sit lower on the shelf. Adding a set confirms in place: the page does not reload or jump, and a "Start studying" bar appears.
- As a learner with an empty library, pressing Study never bounces me somewhere else without a word: Study says there are no cards yet and offers two ways forward, pick a starter set or create cards with AI.
- [S106-1] **Landing hero and Study Specimen:** As a newcomer, the landing hero's Study Specimen steps one card from prompt to typed answer to judgment, on its own or when I pick a step, holds still while I hover or focus it, and shows the judged step still without script or under reduced motion. Its four schedule facts each sit beside an icon on one line, never wrapped, clipped or pressed against a border. Four ruled rows below, each with its icon, say how Scry works.
- [S106-2] **Create from a topic, Markdown or prose:** As a learner, I can tell Study (recall what is due) from Create (new cards with AI). In Create I type a topic, question or book title, paste Markdown, prose or notes (up to 16,000 characters), or tap a starter example under the field, then press "Draft cards" (or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter). Scry's drafting agent always writes the cards, shaped to my study profile and drawn from my allowance like any draft, as atomic questions grouped by idea in a draft outline I approve all at once or card by card. Scry never asks me to fill in a front and back by hand. In test mode drafts come from fixed fixtures, with no network call.
- As a learner, I can turn a favorite book into a set of its core ideas.
- [S106-3] **Import and export decks:** As a learner, I can import an Anki `.apkg` (basic and cloze notes), a UTF-8 `.csv`/`.tsv` (`question,answer` or `idea,question,answer[,exact]`, header optional) or a `.md` file (up to 20 MiB, with bounded unzipping; optional idea title) in Create, linked from Library and Settings → Your data. Anki, CSV and structured Markdown (Scry's export, or `---` card blocks split by `?`) go straight into my library at no allowance cost and open the imported idea with a plain count; unstructured Markdown goes to the drafting agent as a draft outline. HTML becomes plain text; media is skipped. In Settings → Your data I can download everything as Markdown, CSV or JSON on any plan state, and a Markdown or CSV export imports back with every idea, question, answer and exact flag intact.
- [S106-4] **No Inbox; the API adds to the library:** As a learner with my own scripts or tools, I can create an API token in Settings; cards they send with `POST /api/v1/items` (1–50 per request: `question`, `answer`, `idea`, `summary`, `exact`) go straight into my library, and `GET /api/v1/ideas` lists my ideas. There is no Inbox in navigation, headers, keys or empty states; `/inbox` redirects (303) to Library, and old suggestions never strand me in a dead queue. Every token may add, even one made earlier without that permission, and `POST /api/v1/suggestions` still works, adding straight to the library.
- [S106-5] **Manage a card without leaving Study:** As a learner, "Edit card" in the review changes the question, correct answer or idea (existing or newly named); it saves to my library at once and the review shows the new text and place, while the grade just given and its schedule stay. "Snooze" puts a card away until tomorrow in my time zone: before I answer it records no review and leaves the schedule untouched; after, its next review is at least tomorrow. "Delete" removes it, restorable for 30 days in Settings → Recently removed (which lists removed ideas and cards). Snooze and Delete work while a card waits or in its review, and go straight to the next card (or session end) with one quiet line. A waiting card never shows its answer.
- [S106-6] **Nothing else changes; every change is proved headed:** As Phaedrus, I keep everything else: Clerk sign-in with verification, the capped no-card trial, $5 billing and top-ups, model grading, Ask Why?, voice answers, the spoiler-safe waiting screen and bridge cards. Any change made to match this file is verified in a real headed browser (`xvfb-run`, desktop Chromium) at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark, with no console, page or CSP errors and nothing scrolling sideways: the landing Specimen (no clipped, wrapped or crowded fact; stepping; still under reduced motion); Create from Markdown or prose to approval, with no hand-written card form; `.apkg`, CSV and Markdown import and Markdown, CSV and JSON export round-trip; five-destination navigation, `/inbox` redirecting, and `/api/v1/ideas`, `/api/v1/items` and `/api/v1/suggestions` working; Snooze, Edit card and Delete with restore inside Study. After deploy, https://scry.mistystep.io is checked from the credentialed host: `/healthz` is 200 with the deployed `build_sha`, the landing hero renders in both modes on desktop and phone, and existing decks are intact.
- As a learner, I open Scry, get what's due, answer by typing or speaking, and I'm done in about 5 minutes.
- As a learner, nothing on the Study screen gives the answer away before I answer: while a card is waiting, the screen names only its topic and a neutral set number ("NATO phonetic alphabet · Set 1"), never an idea title or any label that holds a word of the answer to this card or to the cards beside it.
- As a learner, every answer I submit (correct, almost correct, incorrect, or an exact text) stops on a review of that answer before the next card: what I wrote beside the card's correct answer, a verdict in words (Correct, Almost Correct, Incorrect), the one-line reason, and "Next card" when I am ready. Scry never skips ahead on its own, and the review always stays framed at the question and judgment—never scrolling the question out of view. The review shows only the card I just answered: no earlier card above it and no glimpse of the next card below it, and on my phone nothing in it hides behind the "Next card" bar. If I submit nothing, the review says "No answer given." and, when the grader marks it Incorrect, "Skipped — review the answer above." (an empty answer is judged by the grader like any other; Scry has no local verdict for it).
- As a learner, in that review I see this card's real schedule, read from what Scry has stored: when it is next due (the date, and how long from now on the same clock as the interval, so "today" never sits beside a one-day interval), its current interval (the gap from this answer to that review), its stability and difficulty, and how often I have reviewed it and with what verdicts. These are facts, not scores: no streaks, points or celebration.
- As a learner, I never grade myself. Every non-exact answer is graded correct, almost correct or incorrect on understanding, not case, punctuation or trivialities, by a decisions-model grader. Every answer is judged instantly with a one-line reason in plain, human words, drawn from the criterion the grader applied; the grader's internal rubric wording never reaches the screen. After any model-graded answer I can press "Ask Why?" and get a short explanation: what differed when it was almost correct or incorrect, or why it holds when it was correct. Asking never changes the grade or the schedule, and if no explanation can be had the reason is shown instead. Texts I mark exact ignore case, punctuation and apostrophes, then are judged word for word. Near misses show a word-by-word diff.
- As a learner who keeps missing a card, Scry notices it from what it has stored, never from a guess: a card with 3 or more lapses, or whose latest two graded answers were both Incorrect, says "Repeated miss — scaffold prerequisite bridges?" in its post-answer review, on its own page and in its deck list (never while it waits for an answer). "Build bridge cards" asks the same drafting model as Create for two or three atomic prerequisite cards, shown with the card's question and my recent misses in mind; I can edit or remove any of them in place, and nothing is added until I press "Add bridges & hold parent". The bridges then join the card's idea and the card is held out of every due queue ("Held by 2 bridges") until each bridge is answered Correct or Almost Correct once, when it returns on its own schedule, unchanged. An Incorrect on a bridge keeps the hold. I can "Release hold" by hand at any time; removing a bridge card ends that bridge, and trashing the idea pauses the hold until it is restored. Exact texts are not offered bridges. A drafted bridge never gives the card away: one that repeats its question or contains its whole answer (as words, or literally when the answer is only symbols, like "!=") is dropped before I see the preview, and if fewer than two remain Scry says it couldn't build them. Every card in the preview fits a card's limits, so it can be added unchanged; a stale "Release hold" sent after the idea went to Trash changes nothing. In test mode bridges always come from a fixed fixture, with no model call.
- As a learner, I can see how ideas connect, and each item belongs to an idea. On phones, opening an idea shows that idea and its cards immediately above a compact collapsible outline.
- As a learner on my phone, every destination (Study, Library, Create, Billing, Settings) is in view at once in one compact navigation row, and Study does not repeat its own name and purpose under the navigation that already says I am in Study.
- As a learner, my library and history are mine alone, and nobody else can see them.
- As a paying user, I know what I pay and what my allowance covers, and I can top up or cancel any time.

- As an operator, production errors and health are watched continuously, and an agent is alerted, triages and proposes or ships the fix. A fault drill proves this works.

## Business rules

- No free tier. There's a 7-day interactive trial with no card required up front, guarded by a strict capped AI usage allowance (judging, speaking, explanations and drafting share it; no top-ups without a card). Access ends when the 7 days do. Upgrade any time in Billing or on the card step to the $5 a month plan: with a card added during the trial, the first charge is when it ends; in the trial's last hour or after it, the plan starts and is charged at once, with no second trial. Scry never charges today unless the page the learner submitted said so: if the trial reached its last hour (or ended) after the page was loaded, nothing is charged and the learner is asked to confirm the charge first. A declined card never shortens the trial.
- Prices cover costs plus a small margin, only to protect against abuse and runaway costs: a small monthly base with a capped allowance, and top-ups beyond it. Top-ups cost $5 each, up to $20 per person per calendar month (UTC); the limit resets at the start of each UTC month. One press of Top up is one charge, however often the form is resubmitted.
- Phaedrus is the first customer, with no special powers in the product.
- Reliable from day one: outages are noticed and fixed without anyone being asked.

## Feel

Calm, precise and warm, in the style of Warp and Supermemory. No game mechanics. Dark and light modes. The details live in DESIGN.md.

## Not now

Sharing, public decks, classrooms, streaks, importing from links, images or video, and predicting performance.
