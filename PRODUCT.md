# Scry

Scry gets what's worth knowing into your head and keeps it there, for the fewest minutes a day. It is built for Phaedrus and usable by anyone.

## User stories

- As a newcomer, I can understand at a glance that Scry is an AI-judged active-recall instrument, see one real example of a card being judged on the landing page, sign up and sign in through Clerk with email and password, and be studying within a minute: the 7-day trial starts at once, with no card up front. I can also add Scry to my phone's home screen to launch directly into daily recall.
- As a learner on the trial, I always know how many days are left, and I can add a card any time in Billing or on the card step to keep Scry after it; adding one during the trial charges nothing until the trial ends. When the trial ends or its allowance runs out, Scry says so calmly and shows me where to add a card.
- As a newcomer, I must verify my email before I can study or use billing.
- As a returning learner who forgot my password, I enter my email on Sign in, choose "Forgot password" on the password step to reset it by email, and sign in with my new password.
- As a new user with an empty library, I can pick from about 30 excellent starter sets and start right away. The shelf leads with sets anyone can enjoy at once (the NATO phonetic alphabet, mental models, kitchen numbers, world geography), then core ideas in every major subject and vocabulary; the Creeds and the other word-for-word texts sit lower on the shelf. Adding a set confirms in place: the page does not reload or jump, and a "Start studying" bar appears.
- As a learner with an empty library, pressing Study never bounces me somewhere else without a word: Study says there are no cards yet and offers two ways forward, pick a starter set or create cards with AI.
- [S106-1] **Landing hero and Study Specimen:** As a newcomer, the landing hero's Study Specimen steps one card from prompt to typed answer to judgment, on its own or when I pick a step, holds still while I hover or focus it, and shows the judged step still without script or under reduced motion. Its four schedule facts each sit beside an icon on one line, never wrapped, clipped or pressed against a border. Four ruled rows below, each with its icon, say how Scry works.
- [S106-2] **Create from a topic, Markdown or prose:** As a learner, I can tell Study (recall what is due) from Create (new cards with AI). In Create I type a topic, question or book title, paste Markdown, prose or notes (up to 16,000 characters), or tap a starter example under the field, then press "Draft cards" (or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter). Scry's drafting agent always writes the cards, shaped to my study profile and drawn from my allowance like any draft, as atomic questions grouped by idea in a draft outline I approve all at once or card by card. Scry never asks me to fill in a front and back by hand. In test mode drafts come from fixed fixtures, with no network call.
- As a learner, I can turn a favorite book into a set of its core ideas.
- [S106-3] **Import and export decks:** As a learner, I can import an Anki `.apkg` (basic and cloze notes), a UTF-8 `.csv`/`.tsv` (`question,answer` or `idea,question,answer[,exact]`, header optional) or a `.md` file (up to 20 MiB, with bounded unzipping; optional idea title) in Create, linked from Library and Settings → Your data. Anki, CSV and structured Markdown (Scry's export, or `---` card blocks split by `?`) go straight into my library at no allowance cost and open the imported idea with a plain count; unstructured Markdown goes to the drafting agent as a draft outline. HTML becomes plain text; media is skipped. In Settings → Your data I can download everything as Markdown, CSV or JSON on any plan state, and a Markdown or CSV export imports back with every idea, question, answer and exact flag intact.
- [S106-4] **No Inbox; the API adds to the library:** As a learner with my own scripts or tools, I can create an API token in Settings; cards they send with `POST /api/v1/items` (1–50 per request: `question`, `answer`, `idea`, `summary`, `exact`) go straight into my library, and `GET /api/v1/ideas` lists my ideas. There is no Inbox in navigation, headers, keys or empty states; `/inbox` redirects (303) to Library, and old suggestions never strand me in a dead queue. Every token may add, even one made earlier without that permission, and `POST /api/v1/suggestions` still works, adding straight to the library.
- [S106-5] **Manage a card without leaving Study:** As a learner, "Edit card" in the review changes the question, correct answer or idea (existing or newly named); it saves to my library at once and the review shows the new text and place, while the grade just given and its schedule stay. "Snooze" puts a card away until tomorrow in my time zone: before I answer it records no review and leaves the schedule untouched; after, its next review is at least tomorrow. "Delete" removes it, restorable for 30 days in Settings → Recently removed (which lists removed ideas and cards). Snooze and Delete work while a card waits or in its review, and go straight to the next card (or session end) with one quiet line. A waiting card never shows its answer.
- [S106-6] **Nothing else changes; every change is proved headed:** Except for the changes in [S111-1] through [S111-3], [S113-1] through [S113-3] and [S125-1], as Phaedrus I keep everything else: Clerk sign-in with verification, the capped no-card trial, $5 billing and top-ups, model grading, Ask Why?, voice answers, the spoiler-safe waiting screen and bridge cards. Any change made to match this file is verified in a real headed browser (`xvfb-run`, desktop Chromium) at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark, with no console, page or CSP errors and nothing scrolling sideways: the landing Specimen (no clipped, wrapped or crowded fact; stepping; still under reduced motion); Create from Markdown or prose to approval, with no hand-written card form; `.apkg`, CSV and Markdown import and Markdown, CSV and JSON export round-trip; five-destination navigation, `/inbox` redirecting, and `/api/v1/ideas`, `/api/v1/items` and `/api/v1/suggestions` working; Snooze, Edit card and Delete with restore inside Study. After deploy, https://scry.mistystep.io is checked from the credentialed host: `/healthz` is 200 with the deployed `build_sha`, the landing hero renders in both modes on desktop and phone, and existing decks are intact.
- [S111-1] **Punch in and study: one interleaved session across every deck:** As a learner, opening `/study` (or pressing Study in the navigation, or `g s`) starts studying at once across all my active decks (each top-level idea or starter set is a deck); I never pick a deck or subject first. A session holds what fits its five-minute budget: an ordinary card counts 20 seconds and an exact-text line counts 30 seconds, so a session holds at most 15 cards (fewer when exact lines are in it), and starts only when at least one card is eligible. The session considers up to 200 due review candidates and reserves room for due reviews first, most urgent first (lowest current FSRS retrievability, then earliest due). It fills only the room left with new cards I am ready to see, oldest first, at most 10 first presentations a day in my stored time zone across all sessions, focused or interleaved. From those cards Scry chooses the most urgent due review from a different deck than the last card, or a paced new card from a different deck if there is no such review. It repeats the last deck only when no other deck has an eligible card, giving due reviews priority within that deck too. A new card used to break a streak never takes a review's place in the session, and the eligible lines of an exact text keep their order. Each waiting card uses its spoiler-safe place line ("NATO phonetic alphabet · Set 1") as a quiet deck tag when one can be shown without giving away an answer. Studying one deck is still possible but never in the way: on an idea's page in Library, a quiet "Study this idea" starts a session over only that idea and its sub-ideas, under the same rules. **Proved headed:** with due reviews of known FSRS urgency and new cards spread unevenly across at least three demo decks, both `/study` and Study in the navigation open directly on a card. The first card is the most urgent due review, no two consecutive cards use the same deck while another deck has an eligible card, and the most urgent eligible review from another deck wins over a new card. A paced new card can break a streak when only the previous deck has due reviews left; a backlog of 15 or more eligible due reviews, all ordinary cards, fills the session (15 cards, all due reviews) and leaves no room for new cards. With exactly 10 ordinary due reviews, at least 5 eligible ordinary new cards, and at least 5 first presentations left in the daily limit, the session contains 10 due reviews and 5 new cards. With more than 10 new cards ready, reopening Study or switching to focused study on the same learner day still allows no more than 10 first presentations total. Waiting cards show only safe tags (a neutral tag or no tag when needed), and "Study this idea" in Library opens a session holding only that idea and its sub-ideas.
- [S111-2] **Write a full answer: a multiline answer field that grows:** As a learner, I answer in an auto-expanding multiline `<textarea>`, not a one-line input, so I can write several sentences or a paragraph. It grows one row at a time as I type or dictate, wraps words at the field's edge, never scrolls or clips sideways, and only past 45% of the window's height scrolls inside itself. Enter checks my answer, as before; Shift+Enter starts a new line; on desktop a quiet hint says so ("Enter to check · Shift+Enter for new line · Space to start/stop speaking (empty answer)"). On a touch keyboard (390×844) Return checks my answer, a long answer wraps onto new rows as one paragraph, line breaks I paste or dictate are kept (a hardware keyboard's Shift+Enter still starts a new line), and the hint under the field reads "Return to check". A spoken answer is added to what I already typed and the field grows to fit it. **Proved headed:** at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark, typing a three-sentence answer and then a paragraph grows the field row by row with no horizontal scroll or clipped text (the field's `scrollWidth` never exceeds its `clientWidth`, and the page never scrolls sideways); the labelled field exposes the key hint to assistive technology (the desktop hint at 1440×900, "Return to check" at 390×844); with a hardware keyboard, Shift+Enter inserts a newline and does not submit; at phone size the answer field has `enterkeyhint="send"` and Return checks the answer; submitting a multiline answer preserves its line breaks in the review, including pasted or dictated breaks; a very long answer stops growing at `max-height: 45vh` and scrolls inside the field; a dictated answer (from the test speech adapter) is appended after typed text and the field re-measures its height.
- [S111-3] [S113-1] **Long explanations get a semantic verdict, from models only:** As a learner, a multi-sentence or paragraph-long explanation of a concept is judged as reliably as a short answer: I get Correct, Almost Correct or Incorrect with its one-line reason and my schedule moves, never a "Couldn't check that just now" dead end because the grader could not decide. **Models only, never heuristics:** every non-exact answer is judged by a decisions model or a call to a capable LLM, never by fuzzy string matching, token overlap, keywords, regular expressions or embedding or cosine-similarity thresholds, because no heuristic holds across arbitrary quizzes and conceptual answers; even a non-exact answer identical to the correct answer is judged by a model. Only cards marked exact are compared word for word. **One ordered grading chain:** (1) the primary decisions model, `typesafe/jev-1.14` (Jev); (2) if it is unavailable, errors or returns something that cannot be read, the next decisions model in the configured fallback chain, in order, by default `openai/gpt-6-luna-decisions` then `google/gemini-decision-1`; (3) as soon as any decisions model answers with low certainty (below its confidence, probability or margin bars, or "none of these"), or the whole chain gives no confident verdict, Scry escalates once to a fast, capable LLM, by default `google/gemini-4-hydrogen` (Gemini 4 Hydrogen), with the same question, accepted answers, rubric and structured three-way output, so a valid conceptual answer resolves cleanly to Correct, Almost Correct or Incorrect and its verdict, reason and schedule are recorded the same way. A low-certainty verdict goes straight to the LLM; it is never shopped to another decisions model. Every model is a real OpenRouter slug on `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`: `SCRY_JUDGE_MODEL` overrides the primary, `SCRY_JUDGE_FALLBACK_MODELS` (comma-separated, may be empty) the fallback chain, and `SCRY_JUDGE_ESCALATION_MODEL` the LLM; on the OpenRouter route a value that is not a `vendor/model` slug stops the boot. Without an OpenRouter key, the chain is Jev on its direct Typesafe route and the LLM is the configured drafting model on its own provider and credential. Each attempt is logged with its requested and resolved model, latency, cost and outcome, never the answer text or a key. **One charge:** one submission counts as at most one judgment against my allowance: a single judgment reservation, sized for the costliest path the chain may take, covers it whether one model or several were asked, so it is never charged twice. Only when grading cannot complete (no model in the chain or the escalation returns a usable verdict, or the request is interrupted before a review is recorded) does Study say "Couldn't check that just now. Your answer is still here; press Enter to try again.", with my typed or dictated answer, line breaks included, still in the field, so no work is lost. If a review was recorded but the response was lost, retrying opens that review without another charge or schedule update. Allowance limits still say so in their own words. In test mode every step of the chain and the escalation is a fixed fixture, with no network call. **Proved headed and by test:** submit multi-sentence and paragraph-long conceptual answers with known Correct, Almost Correct and Incorrect fixture outcomes; each shows the expected verdict and reason and updates the stored FSRS schedule. With the primary forced unavailable, errored and unparseable in turn (test adapter), the next decisions model in the chain answers; with every decisions model unavailable, the escalation LLM answers; with the primary forced to low confidence, low probability, a thin margin or "none of these", the escalation LLM answers and no secondary decisions model is asked; each path produces the same outcomes and no response is a `503`. A non-exact answer identical to the correct answer still reaches a model, and no code path grades a non-exact answer without one. With every grading path failing, or with the request interrupted before a review is recorded, the same card keeps the complete typed or dictated answer in the field, line breaks included; after recovery Enter retries and a verdict updates the stored FSRS schedule; if the response is lost after the review is recorded, retrying opens that review with no second charge or schedule update; a submission that walks the whole chain and escalates is charged to the allowance as exactly one judgment (one judgment reservation, never two).
- [S113-2] **Every wait and every change is shown, calmly:** As a learner, nothing I do leaves an ambiguous pause or a silent failure. Checking an answer ("checking" after 400 ms, "still checking" after 3 s while a fallback or escalated check runs), transcribing a dictation, saving a card edit, importing or exporting a deck, snoozing or deleting a card each say what is happening in a quiet mono word beside the control that started it, with that control disabled so a second press does nothing; each failure says what went wrong in words, keeps everything I typed, chose or dictated exactly as it was, and lets me try again in place. Every action and state change (press, hover, focus, the answer field growing, the checking pulse, the verdict and struck answer appearing, the next card arriving, switching destination, Edit card opening and closing, a snoozed or deleted card leaving, status lines, an error) moves with one restrained, deliberate animation of 120–240 ms, ease-out (only the checking pulse repeats, slowly, until the verdict arrives), never bouncing, overshooting or making me wait; with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it all appears at once or with a plain opacity fade, and every state still reads in words. **Proved headed:** at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark, each of those waits shows its word and disabled control, each forced failure (test adapters) keeps the input intact and recovers on retry, every listed transition's computed duration is 120–240 ms with an ease-out curve and no overshoot (and a recorded walkthrough shows them), and under emulated reduced motion no element translates, scales or pulses while every state is still named.
- [S113-3] [S125-1] **One mark everywhere: the L3-S3 fanned deck with its cobalt top card tilted:** As anyone who sees Scry, the same brand mark meets me in the browser tab, the header and on my home screen, exactly as Phaedrus approved it in Visual Lab 1: three solid portrait playing cards fanned up and to the right, the top one in Signal Cobalt tilted 10° clockwise with a light prompt bar, beside the lowercase `scry` wordmark. Every surface draws it from the one canonical SVG in DESIGN.md ("Brand mark and wordmark"), verbatim, so nothing drifts: the SVG favicon, the 16 and 32 px PNG favicons, the 24 px header mark and the 64, 180, 192 and 512 px app icons. It replaces the earlier lens mark and the interim flat, upright, landscape outline deck everywhere (favicon, header, app icon, social preview). **Proved headed:** the 16 px and 32 px favicons, the 24 px header mark beside `scry` and the 64 px app icon render crisply in light and dark at 1440×900 and 390×844, with three portrait cards, solid layered slabs, the tilted cobalt top card and its prompt bar distinguishable at each size and nothing clipped.
- As a learner, I open Scry, press Study, get what's due across all my decks, answer by typing (a word or a paragraph) or speaking, and I'm done in about 5 minutes.
- As a learner, nothing on the Study screen gives the answer away before I answer: while a card is waiting, the screen names only its topic and a neutral set number ("NATO phonetic alphabet · Set 1"), never an idea title or any label that holds a word of the answer to this card or to the cards beside it.
- As a learner, every graded answer (correct, almost correct, incorrect, or an exact text) stops on a review of that answer before the next card: what I wrote beside the card's correct answer, a verdict in words (Correct, Almost Correct, Incorrect), the one-line reason, and "Next card" when I am ready. Scry never skips ahead on its own, and the review always stays framed at the question and judgment—never scrolling the question out of view. The review shows only the card I just answered: no earlier card above it and no glimpse of the next card below it, and on my phone nothing in it hides behind the "Next card" bar. If I submit nothing, the review says "No answer given." and, when the grader marks it Incorrect, "Skipped — review the answer above." (an empty answer is judged by the grader like any other; Scry has no local verdict for it).
- As a learner, in that review I see this card's real schedule, read from what Scry has stored: when it is next due (the date, and how long from now on the same clock as the interval, so "today" never sits beside a one-day interval), its current interval (the gap from this answer to that review), its stability and difficulty, and how often I have reviewed it and with what verdicts. These are facts, not scores: no streaks, points or celebration.
- As a learner, I never grade myself. Every non-exact answer is graded correct, almost correct or incorrect on understanding, not case, punctuation or trivialities, by models alone: a decisions model (Jev first, then its fallback chain), with a fast, capable LLM judging whenever no decisions model is certain ([S113-1]); never by a fuzzy, keyword or similarity heuristic. Every verdict comes with a one-line reason in plain, human words, drawn from the criterion the grader applied; the grader's internal rubric wording never reaches the screen. After any model-graded answer I can press "Ask Why?" and get a short explanation: what differed when it was almost correct or incorrect, or why it holds when it was correct. Asking never changes the grade or the schedule, and if no explanation can be had the reason is shown instead. Texts I mark exact ignore case, punctuation and apostrophes, then are judged word for word. Near misses show a word-by-word diff.
- As a learner who keeps missing a card, Scry notices it from what it has stored, never from a guess: a card with 3 or more lapses, or whose latest two graded answers were both Incorrect, says "Repeated miss — scaffold prerequisite bridges?" in its post-answer review, on its own page and in its deck list (never while it waits for an answer). "Build bridge cards" asks the same drafting model as Create for two or three atomic prerequisite cards, shown with the card's question and my recent misses in mind; I can edit or remove any of them in place, and nothing is added until I press "Add bridges & hold parent". The bridges then join the card's idea and the card is held out of every due queue ("Held by 2 bridges") until each bridge is answered Correct or Almost Correct once, when it returns on its own schedule, unchanged. An Incorrect on a bridge keeps the hold. I can "Release hold" by hand at any time; removing a bridge card ends that bridge, and trashing the idea pauses the hold until it is restored. Exact texts are not offered bridges. A drafted bridge never gives the card away: one that repeats its question or contains its whole answer (as words, or literally when the answer is only symbols, like "!=") is dropped before I see the preview, and if fewer than two remain Scry says it couldn't build them. Every card in the preview fits a card's limits, so it can be added unchanged; a stale "Release hold" sent after the idea went to Trash changes nothing. In test mode bridges always come from a fixed fixture, with no model call.
- As a learner, I can see how ideas connect, and each item belongs to an idea. On phones, opening an idea shows that idea and its cards immediately above a compact collapsible outline.
- As a learner on my phone, every destination (Study, Library, Create, Billing, Settings) is in view at once in one compact navigation row, and Study does not repeat its own name and purpose under the navigation that already says I am in Study.
- As a learner, my library and history are mine alone, and nobody else can see them.
- As a paying user, I know what I pay and what my allowance covers, and I can top up or cancel any time.

- As an operator, production errors and health are watched continuously, and an agent is alerted, triages and proposes or ships the fix. A fault drill proves this works.

## Business rules

- No free tier. There's a 7-day interactive trial with no card required up front, guarded by a strict capped AI usage allowance (judging, speaking, explanations and drafting share it; no top-ups without a card). Access ends when the 7 days do. Upgrade any time in Billing or on the card step to the $5 a month plan: with a card added during the trial, the first charge is when it ends; in the trial's last hour or after it, the plan starts and is charged at once, with no second trial. Scry never charges today unless the page the learner submitted said so: if the trial reached its last hour (or ended) after the page was loaded, nothing is charged and the learner is asked to confirm the charge first. A declined card never shortens the trial.
- Prices cover costs plus a small margin, only to protect against abuse and runaway costs: a small monthly base with a capped allowance, and top-ups beyond it. Top-ups cost $5 each, up to $20 per person per calendar month (UTC); the limit resets at the start of each UTC month. One press of Top up is one charge, however often the form is resubmitted.
- Phaedrus is the first customer, with no special powers in the product.
- Reliable from day one: outages are noticed and fixed without anyone being asked.

## Feel

Calm, precise and warm, in the style of Warp and Supermemory. No game mechanics. Dark and light modes. The details live in DESIGN.md.

## Not now

Sharing, public decks, classrooms, streaks, importing from links, images or video, and predicting performance.
